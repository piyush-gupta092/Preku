Productivity

Know what's next, and start it in one click.

Your tasks and today's recap are right in the notch. Star up to three priorities, set a reminder, and start a focus session on any task.

  • Quick capture: a shortcut opens the notch ready to type
  • Focus minutes count toward the task you're working on
  • Clipboard history with pins, and a private mode
Wellbeing

Gentle nudges, never nagging.

Breaks arrive at slightly unpredictable moments so they don't feel mechanical. They count only the time you're actually at the PC, and wait while you focus or present.

  • Water, habits with streaks, bedtime and meals
  • Screen time by app, kept on your PC
  • A short weekly reflection, with no scores and no guilt
A moment for you

The first time you open the notch each day, Pip says hello, mentions the weather, and offers a thought to carry with you. Save it, or dismiss it until tomorrow.

Skills

Make Preku yours, and share it.

A skill is one small file that adds thoughts, nudges or short sounds. Anyone can make one in a few minutes and share it with everyone.

Skills can't run code, read your data or go online. They only add words and sounds, so they're safe to install from anywhere.

All versions and release notes

  1. Run the Preku setup file you downloaded. Preku isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. Pip appears at the top of your screen. Click or pull it down to open the notch, or press Ctrl + Alt + Space.
  3. Open Settings (the gear) to pick what shows in your notch, and turn on Launch Preku when I sign in.

Questions

Is Preku free?

Yes. Preku is free and open source under the MIT license.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Code-signing certificates are expensive and Preku is a young independent project, so the installer isn't signed yet. Windows SmartScreen shows a warning for unsigned apps. You can check the source code and build it yourself from GitHub.

Does Preku send my data anywhere?

No. There's no account and no telemetry. Preku only goes online for things you turn on: the weather for a city you choose, a calendar link you add, or GitHub builds and websites you ask it to watch.

Will it get in the way while I work?

Preku stays a thin pill until you need it. Reminders wait during focus sessions, games and presentations, and you can pause everything from the tray.

Mac or Linux?

Windows is the main platform today. A Linux build is in progress. There's no Mac version yet.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Preku → Uninstall. In Preku, Settings → General can also export or delete all your data first.
